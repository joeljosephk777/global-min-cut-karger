The algorithm

Repeatedly merge a random edge until two “supernodes” remain.

Contract(G):
  while more than 2 supernodes:
     pick a uniformly random edge (u,v)
     merge u,v into one node
        · keep parallel edges  (multigraph)
        · delete self-loops
  return edges between the final 2 supernodes  ← a cut

Why it works (analysis)

Empirical check — Monte Carlo

A single run often fails — so you repeat it and keep the smallest cut found.
